Gulf Chronic Disease Watch
Data project, WHO data

Gulf Chronic Disease Watch

How diabetes, obesity, high blood pressure and early chronic disease deaths have moved across the six GCC states and India since 2000.

  • Built on the WHO Global Health Observatory API, with all 35 data series checksum-verified.
  • Calls a change "clear" only when WHO's uncertainty ranges don't overlap. That surfaced 9 clear shifts, led by obesity.
  • Adds a pandemic anomaly check, a payer lens on diabetes treatment, and seven data quirks written up in plain language.
