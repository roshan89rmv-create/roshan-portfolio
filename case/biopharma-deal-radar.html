Explains who is buying whom in medicine across the USA, India and the Gulf, for people outside the industry.

The problem

Pharma deal news is written for investors, with terms such as "up to $7B", ADCs and in vivo CAR-T.

People moving into health tech, life sciences or payer roles can't easily see the patterns: who is buying, what they want, and why it matters for patients and costs.

What I built

A filterable board of 80+ pharma and biotech deals that adds new deals every Monday.

  • Deal cards that say who did what, what the company makes and why it matters, filterable by region, type and health area.
  • Charts of the biggest deals and busiest buyers, plus a plain-English table of who competes with whom.
  • A quiz, talking points and a word list, so readers can use what they learn in a conversation.

Built with: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, embedded JSON data, a scheduled weekly research task, Claude

Open Biopharma Deal Radar

How I approached it

  • UserNon-specialists preparing for interviews and conversations. That's why it has talking points and a quiz, not just a data table.
  • ConstraintsHeadline deal values often include future "up to" payments that may never be paid. The page says so, and the word list explains upfront versus "up to".
  • Trade-offsI narrowed it to three regions, the USA, India and the UAE and Gulf, and removed Europe, so each region stays deep enough to be useful.

What it shows about me

I can translate a technical market into plain language without losing accuracy.
